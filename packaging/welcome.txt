Less PA

Less PA cancels PA speaker bleed out of an audience microphone, using the PA
feed itself as a reference signal.

This installer places two plugins in your personal plug-in folders:

  * Audio Unit (AU)   ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Less PA.component
  * VST3              ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Less PA.vst3

Both install into your own home folder, so no administrator password is
needed and nothing is changed system-wide. The Installation Type step lists
both formats, ticked; untick the one you don't need.

Requirements: macOS 13 or newer on Apple Silicon, and a DAW that supports AU
or VST3 plugins with a sidechain / second input bus.


Signing

This installer is signed with an Apple Developer ID (Sounds Good To Me AS)
and notarized by Apple, as are both plugins it installs. Nothing needs to be
approved in System Settings and there is no Terminal step -- it installs, and
the plugins load in your DAW, without any Gatekeeper detour.


After installing

Restart your DAW, or trigger a plugin rescan, so it picks up the new plugin.
In the plugin, wire your PA feed into the Reference input and the audience
mic into the main input. Click the "?" button for the full control reference.
